title: Mission
description: Why AIs@home exists and who runs it.
---

> Distributed AI inference so that the megacorps do not get a total monopoly.

Running the best AI models takes datacentre hardware, and that concentrates AI in a few
companies. Open-weight models changed half of that: anyone may run them. AIs@home tries the
other half: serving them from volunteers' GPUs to people who don't have expensive GPUs
themselves.

It is an **experiment**: we don’t know yet whether volunteer GPUs can serve AI well enough,
safely enough and cheaply enough. The way to find out is to build it in the open and
measure.

## Who runs it

One volunteer maintainer. AIs@home is non-commercial: no company, no investors,
no token, no ads. The software is open source under Apache-2.0; the name
“AIs@home” and the logo are not licensed.
