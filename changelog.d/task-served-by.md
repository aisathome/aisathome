- **Each proved theorem's page shows its tasks and who served them.** The page of a proved theorem
  lists the leaf's tasks in the trial that proved it (each outline, hole and assembly, with its
  result, steps, model minutes and tokens) and which GPUs did each task's model work. The gateway's
  public task list has a new `served_by`: per task, the nodes that served its model calls, named
  only by their owner's public username, otherwise unnamed, from the usage log, so older tasks
  have it too as far back as the log names them. A public username is one opt-in: it names your
  account on the leaderboard and in the BOINC statistics, and your nodes on the proof pages; the
  account page, the credit page and the privacy page say so. (gateway, site)
